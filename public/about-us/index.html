How These Tools Work (PWAs)

Most of the tools I build are designed as Progressive Web Apps (PWAs). What does this mean for you?

  • Downloads Once: When you first visit the site, the application downloads from the server directly to your browser.
  • Runs Locally: Once downloaded, the entire application runs locally on your own device.
  • Offline Capable: Because it runs locally, you can continue to use the tool even if you lose your internet connection.
  • Maximum Privacy: Running locally means your sensitive data (like files, logs, or private keys) stays on your device and is never transmitted back to my servers.
