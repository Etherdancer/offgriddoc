OffGridDoc - Offline Medical Document Vault

OffGridDoc is a critical emergency preparedness tool. It allows you to store, view, and organize crucial medical documents, identification, and survival manuals in a secure, completely offline environment. Designed for survivalists and preppers facing grid-down scenarios.

Features

  • Functions 100% without internet access after installation
  • Locally encrypted data storage
  • Fast PDF and Image viewing
